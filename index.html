Model source: Jain, N. (2025). A Study of Repo Rate’s Impact on The Indian Stock Market. Educational Administration: Theory and Practice, 31(1), 748-751. https://doi.org/10.67145/kuey.v31i1.9983 (CC BY 4.0)

How the model and data work

The paper’s log-log regression is ln(Nifty) = α + β₁ln(Repo) + β₂ln(GDP) + β₃ln(CPI), fitted on 24 annual observations (March 2001 to March 2024) with adjusted R² of 94.9%. The paper reports the three coefficients but not the intercept α or the raw data, so α here is calibrated so that the model fits the six historical snapshots on average. Because of that, absolute levels are indicative; the sensitivities to each input come directly from the paper.

Snapshot data: Nifty closes and repo rates are 31 March values. GDP is approximate World Bank nominal GDP for the calendar year. CPI is CPI-IW (fiscal-year average) linked to a 1960 base using official linking factors, so treat it as approximate.
